# Parley SSH ideas

> Ideas for Parley SSH that might become requirements later, above all what goes beyond PuTTY. Nothing here is
> planned or promised: the plan is [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). Look an idea up with `grep -n` (`"| IDEA-37 "`) rather
> than reading the whole file. The terminal's ideas are in [../IDEAS.md](../IDEAS.md), Parley SQL's in [../sql/IDEAS.md](../sql/IDEAS.md).

## How an idea moves on

- A new idea gets the next free `IDEA-n` from the last line of [../IDEAS.md](../IDEAS.md), which is then moved on: one
  sequence for all three. IDs are never reused or renumbered.
- When an idea is picked for a phase, write it as one or more requirements in the roadmap (§5, next free ID of the
  area) and mark the row here *"→ R-SSH-n"*. Remove the row once its requirements are done; the number stays used.
- An idea that would change a decision says so; the decision is taken before it is built.
